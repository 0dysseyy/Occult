Pulsations (Empreintes).

Music

Statement

Sachie Kobayashi is a composer and media artist working across instrumental sound, electronics, voice, video and interactive systems. Through sound and multimedia, her practice explores questions of human existence and ways of being, cultural and linguistic boundaries, and the relationship between the body and technology.

Her ongoing Trans-Instrumentalism series, which began with Day 0 / Trans-Instrumentalism at IRCAM, questions what happens when the human body itself becomes a musical instrument, and how technology reshapes our understanding of identity, life and death. Moving between sound, language, image and technology, her work examines the shifting boundaries between human and machine, music and noise, and the familiar and the extraordinary.

Her works have been presented internationally, including performances by Klangforum Wien and a VR work exploring Trans-Instrumentalism, created in collaboration with Ensemble Contrechamps and presented at the Geneva International Film Festival (GIFF).
