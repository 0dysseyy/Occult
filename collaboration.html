Submissions & Partnership.

O C C U L T was built to hold various themes from various artists — which means this isn't a one-voice project, and it was never meant to be. If you're making work that doesn't quite fit anywhere else, we want to see it.

This is open to writing, essays, poetry, film, art, photography, and anonymous accounts — one-off submissions for Under the Veil, an ongoing place in the collective itself, or a partnership between O C C U L T and what you're building elsewhere. Tell us what you're working on, and where you'd want it to live.
