Notes

I initially started photography as a traveling hobby, but it became more important for me with time. I have been mainly focused on animals. I like capturing what I think of as "moments between moments": quiet instances that we overlook or consider unimportant. The moments when we're simply sitting, thinking, moving, or existing without really doing anything in particular.

In the future, I'd like to explore this idea through humans, but that's much more challenging because there is a compromise between capturing something genuine and respecting someone's privacy.
